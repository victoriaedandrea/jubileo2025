Participation vs. Bed-Night Capacity

Official Jubilee program counts capture same-day pilgrims, liturgical gatherings, and group visits, whereas ISTAT hotel and short-term rental series measure overnight stays. Keeping both visible avoids overstating hotel occupancy during single-day surges.
